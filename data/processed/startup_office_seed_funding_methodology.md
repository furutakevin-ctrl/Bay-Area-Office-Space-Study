# Startup office-space and seed-funding study

## Scope

- Population: operating startups headquartered or maintaining a physical office in the nine-county Bay Area.
- Unit of analysis: one startup.
- Office-space categories: `shared` or `private`.
- Outcome: the amount of the company's seed round, in U.S. dollars.
- Funding years: 2023 through 2026 inclusive.
- Target sample: 50 startups, including at least 15 shared-office startups.

## Inclusion rules

1. The company must be an operating startup, not an investment fund, landlord, restaurant, or coworking operator.
2. A source must identify a physical Bay Area office or headquarters.
3. A source must support the office classification. A coworking, WeWork, founder workspace, or shared-desk location is `shared`; a dedicated company suite or conventional company-leased office is `private`.
4. A source must identify a seed round and its amount and year. Pre-seed, Series A, grants, and total lifetime funding are not substituted for seed funding.
5. If a source reports a range or only total funding, the observation is held out until a seed-specific amount is found.

## Analysis

The primary comparison will be the distribution of seed funding for shared versus private offices. Report medians and interquartile ranges, because funding amounts are highly right-skewed. A log-transformed amount can be used for a sensitivity check.

This is an observational comparison and should be described as an association, not a causal effect of office type.
